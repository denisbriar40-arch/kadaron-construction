Project Overview

A complete filling station build from ground up

Kadaron Construction is handling the full construction of this filling station facility. The scope covers the main station structure, the office building including POP ceiling works, and all ancillary works across the site.

This is an active project. The videos on this page show real construction progress captured on site by our team.

Scope of Work

What we are delivering on this project

01

Site Preparation

Full site clearing, levelling and preparation before any structural works commenced.

02

Station Structure

Main filling station canopy and pump island structural works including concrete and steel elements.

03

Office Building

Full construction of the station office building — masonry, roofing, doors, windows and finishes.

04

POP Ceiling Works

Plaster of Paris ceiling installation throughout the office building — frames, boards and finishing.

05

MEP Installations

Electrical wiring, lighting, plumbing and mechanical installations across the full facility.

06

External Works

Driveway, drainage, perimeter fencing and all external civil works around the station.
