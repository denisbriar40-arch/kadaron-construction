Project Overview

Foundation works completed to load requirements and specification

This project required Kadaron Construction to deliver the complete foundation and groundworks package for a development where the soil conditions required careful attention. We coordinated closely with the structural engineer to ensure every element was built to specification.

Foundation work is where shortcuts cause problems ten years later. We do not take shortcuts. Every element was cast, cured and inspected before the superstructure team took over.

Scope of Work

What was delivered on this project

01

Soil Investigation

Coordination of soil testing and review of geotechnical report before design was finalised.

02

Excavation

Bulk excavation and trimming to formation level across the full footprint.

03

Blinding & Waterproofing

Concrete blinding, DPC membranes and full waterproofing system to foundation base.

04

Reinforcement

Steel fixing to structural engineer's specification for all foundation elements.

05

Concrete Works

Foundation concrete poured and cured to correct standards with cube testing.

06

Backfill & Compaction

Engineered backfill and compaction to specified bearing capacity.
