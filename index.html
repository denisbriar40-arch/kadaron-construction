How We Work

How we
run a project

Six stages. Every client goes through the same process, because that is how we keep quality consistent.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We visit the site, talk through what you need, and ask the right questions before we say yes to anything.

  2. 02

    Planning

    We prepare a proper plan materials, labour, timeline and budget before a single brick is laid.

  3. 03

    Site Preparation

    The site is cleared and prepared properly. This is where shortcuts cause problems later, so we do not skip it.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Construction runs under active supervision. Clients get regular updates so there are no surprises.

  5. 05

    Quality Review

    We inspect the work at each stage before moving forward. Defects caught early cost far less to fix.

  6. 06

    Handover

    We walk the finished building with the client, resolve any snags, and hand over with full documentation.
