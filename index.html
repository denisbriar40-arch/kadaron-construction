04 PROCESS

A better build
starts before site work.

Clear decisions early make execution easier later. Our process is designed to keep the scope visible and the next step understood.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Understand the brief, site, priorities and expectations.

  2. 02

    Planning

    Define scope, sequencing, procurement and practical site requirements.

  3. 03

    Preparation

    Coordinate the site and resources before active construction begins.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Execute the agreed work with ongoing coordination and communication.

  5. 05

    Review

    Check work at key stages and address outstanding items.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Walk through the completed scope and close the project properly.
