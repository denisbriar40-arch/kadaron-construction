Project Overview

A full property renovation from structure to finish

The client approached Kadaron Construction with an existing property that needed significant renovation work across every floor. The brief was clear: improve the structure where necessary, update all systems, and deliver a finished property that felt new.

We assessed the building first, identified structural concerns, and addressed them before any cosmetic work began. The result was a fully updated property handed over to a satisfied client.

Scope of Work

What was delivered on this project

01

Structural Assessment

Full survey of existing structure before works commenced to identify all issues.

02

Structural Repairs

Remediation of cracks, reinforcement of weakened elements and waterproofing works.

03

Electrical Rewiring

Full rewiring throughout the property with new consumer unit and fittings.

04

Plumbing Works

Replacement of aged pipework, new bathroom suites and kitchen plumbing.

05

Interior Renovation

New screeding, tiling, plastering, painting and ceiling works throughout.

06

External Renovation

External rendering, painting, gate and fence repairs and driveway works.
